# Synchus — 5 minute stage runbook

## The narrative

**One-line pitch:** Synchus turns files, systems, and frontline experience into a shared operational memory that can explain itself, act cautiously, and improve under human control.

**The hook:** Companies do not lack data. They lack a shared operational memory—and the people closest to the work usually know what the systems missed.

## Before walking on stage

- Open `/` in one tab and `/app/route` in another.
- Keep `/app/inbox`, `/app/approvals`, and `/app/audit` ready in background tabs.
- Confirm the voice orb says ready and the browser has microphone permission.
- Keep the four files in this folder visible in Finder.
- Use Delhi → Ludhiana, Internal, with historical incidents enabled.
- Do one silent rehearsal. Do not pre-upload the files you intend to show; uploads are fingerprinted.

## 0:00–0:35 — Start with the human problem

Say:

> Every company has dashboards. But the truth of an operation is scattered across spreadsheets, tickets, emails—and the driver who knows the bridge is blocked before any system does. Synchus gives the company one living operational memory, without pretending uncertain data is certain.

Show the live orb, then move directly to the route view.

## 0:35–1:25 — Show grounded spatial intelligence

Say:

> This is not live GPS theatre. I select a route, and Synchus compiles what matters for that movement: hubs, registered fleet assignments, historical incidents, client rules, seasonal restrictions, and every missing live signal that blocks a safe decision.

Click a hub, a truck, and a warning. Point out that a truck marker is a home assignment—not a live position. Ask the route assistant:

> What can stop this Delhi to Ludhiana dispatch today, and what do we still not know?

## 1:25–2:20 — Let the ground worker teach the system

Start voice mode and say:

> NH44 par blockage hai. Isko report kar do.

Let Synchus ask for the missing details. Answer naturally:

> Main Rakesh, line-haul driver. Murthal exit se ek kilometre pe northbound lane hai. Abhi 2:10 par dekha. Severity high hai. Shaam 6 baje tak clear hone ka police estimate hai.

When Synchus reads back the report, say:

> Haan, summary sahi hai. Submit kar do.

Say to the audience:

> It did not convert speech into truth. It interviewed the worker, compiled a complete report, and staged evidence for review.

## 2:20–3:15 — Upload data the product has never seen

Open Inbox and upload `02_cold_chain_readings.csv`.

Say:

> Now I am giving Synchus a repeated cold-chain sensor shape that this demo never had a table for. A reckless agent would edit production. Synchus preserves the source, proposes the smallest typed capability, shows which surfaces it affects, and waits.

Open Approvals → Capability Lab. Show fields, source, risk, safety contract, and rollback. Approve only if a proposal was staged; otherwise explain that the agent correctly judged ordinary context sufficient and continue with the next fixture.

## 3:15–4:05 — Show conflict, provenance, and human authority

Upload `04_client_gate_change.md`, then open Approvals.

Say:

> This file contradicts the current Vertex gate rule. Synchus keeps both claims and their sources visible. The agent can recommend; a person decides what becomes canonical.

Open Audit → Events, Capabilities, then Decisions.

> Every model run, raw event, proposal, approval, and rollback remains inspectable. Intelligence without provenance is just confidence theatre.

## 4:05–4:45 — Ask for an operational answer

Return to voice mode and ask:

> DL30AN8381 ko Orion Pharma ke liye Ludhiana bhejna safe hai?

After the answer, say:

> Notice the contract: Synchus retrieves evidence, reconciles conflicts, answers in the worker's language, cites what it used, and says unknown when a live feed is absent.

## 4:45–5:00 — Close

Say:

> Palantir made operational context powerful for institutions. Synchus makes that idea reachable for Indian companies—and lets the people doing the work continuously improve it. Files become evidence. Voices become structured knowledge. Maps become decisions. Humans keep authority.

Pause on the orb and end with:

> Synchus: the company learns at the speed of its ground truth.
